## Context

## ADGS (Advancement Guide System)
ADGS is a guide that detects your surroundings and shows hints in the sidebar for any advancements you can complete.

#### How to use
- Press `Tab` to show the requirement details in the sidebar.
- Press `Tab + Ctrl` to open the details screen. Note the key order: hold Tab first, then press Ctrl.

In the details screen, you can review all advancement hints and their details, even when the sidebar is hidden, and even after you’ve already completed those advancements.
